AI Model Evaluation for SRT Translation

Audit Scope & Methodology

Audit Scope

English-to-Hindi subtitle translation for Interactive Learning Modules (ILMs), evaluated for linguistic quality, pedagogical suitability and subtitle readability.

Evaluation Methodology

Five evaluation dimensions, with overall scores reported out of 100.

Pedagogical Scaffolding
Bridges Hindi text with source English concepts.
Academic Register
Maintains formal, age-appropriate vocabulary suitable for education.
Subtitle Pacing
Assesses subtitle length and reading pace.
Syntactic Accuracy
Ensures grammatical correctness and natural Hindi sentence structure.
Semantic Accuracy
Preserves the meaning, intent, and instructional value of the source text.
